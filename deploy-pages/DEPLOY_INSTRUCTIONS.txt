You have 3 super easy FREE options below. All are 100% free.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OPTION 1 — NETLIFY DROP (RECOMMENDED - 30 seconds, no account needed)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Go to this link in your browser:   https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag the entire "deploy-pages" folder (or just the index.html file) onto the page.
3. Wait 10 seconds.
4. You will get a free public URL like:
   https://amazing-unicorn-42.netlify.app

You can even add a custom domain later for free.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OPTION 2 — MANUAL UPLOAD TO YOUR EXISTING GITHUB REPO (uses your repo)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Go to: https://github.com/antoniocorsano-boop/docentedocai
2. Click the big green button: "Add file" → "Upload files"
3. Drag these two files from the "deploy-pages" folder:
   - index.html
   - .nojekyll
4. At the bottom, change the commit message to: "Publish teacher landing page"
5. Click "Commit changes"
6. Now go to: https://github.com/antoniocorsano-boop/docentedocai/settings/pages
7. Under "Source" choose "Deploy from a branch"
8. Branch: main   Folder: / (root)
9. Click Save
10. Your page will be live at:
    https://antoniocorsano-boop.github.io/docentedocai/

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OPTION 3 — SURGE.SH (if you have Node.js)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Open terminal on your computer
2. Run:
   npm install -g surge
3. cd deploy-pages
4. surge
5. Choose a name like: docentedocai.surge.sh
6. Done! Free URL: https://docentedocai.surge.sh
